Privacy Policy

Find It: Word & Match Puzzle · Last updated: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dedem Labs handles user data in the mobile application Find It: Word & Match Puzzle (package name: com.dedemlabs.finditwordmatchpuzzlegame).

1. Information We Collect

Our app does not request or collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location. You do not need to create an account or sign in to play.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Your game data is stored only in your device's local storage and is never sent to our servers. This includes:

  • Level progress and completed stages
  • In-game diamond balance and purchased power-ups
  • Statistics and earned badges
  • Language, sound, music, and theme preferences
All of this data is deleted when you uninstall the app from your device.

3. Advertising

Our app uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google. In order to serve relevant ads, AdMob may access your device's Advertising ID, IP address, and general device information.

This data is collected and processed directly by Google, not by us. For details, see:

  • Google Advertising Policies
  • Google Privacy Policy

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising at any time from your device settings.

4. In-App Purchases

All in-app purchases are processed entirely through Google Play Billing. Your credit card details and payment information are never shared with us and are not stored by us in any form.

5. Notifications

The app may create local notifications on your device to remind you to come back and play. These notifications are generated on your device and no data is sent to any server. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

6. Photo and Media Access

When you use the card creation feature, the app requests access only to the photo you choose. The selected image is processed solely on your device and is never uploaded to any server or shared with anyone.

7. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that such data has been collected, it will be deleted immediately.

8. Data Security

Because we do not store any of your personal data on our servers, your information is protected by your device's own security measures.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page and the date at the top will be revised accordingly.
